Our Story

Founded in 2021 by a former tech CEO, GhostWriter was born from a simple realization: the most brilliant executives often have the least amount of time to share their insights.

We set out to build a platform that combines elite journalistic talent with cutting-edge SaaS technology, creating a frictionless experience for leaders to scale their personal brands.

Our Mission

To empower 10,000 executives to share knowledge that drives industries forward, without sacrificing their time.

How to Hook B2B Buyers in 3 Lines

Published on May 7, 2026 • 5 min read

The first three lines of your LinkedIn post are arguably the most important real estate on the internet for B2B executives. If you fail to capture attention here, the rest of your brilliant insights will never be seen.

The Anatomy of a Perfect Hook

Most executives start their posts with "I am excited to announce..." or "Today we are launching...". This is a fundamental mistake. The hook is not about you; it's about the reader.

"A great hook agitates a specific problem, challenges a common belief, or teases an unexpected result."

1. Agitate the Problem

Start by describing a pain point your ideal buyer feels every day. Be specific. Instead of "Sales are hard," try "Your SDRs are making 100 calls a day and booking 0 meetings. Here's why."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you scale.

How does the onboarding process work?

After choosing a plan, we schedule a 60-minute discovery call to learn your voice, industry expertise, and content goals. Within 48 hours, you'll receive your first draft of content in the dashboard for approval.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your dashboard. Changes will be reflected in your next billing cycle.

Who actually writes my content?

Your content is written by a dedicated team of elite journalistic talent, vetted specifically for executive ghostwriting. We don't use AI for the core writing; we use it only for research and data analytics.

Is there a long-term commitment?

All our plans are month-to-month by default. However, we do offer annual discounts if you choose to commit to a longer-term brand strategy.
